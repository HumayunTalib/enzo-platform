ENZO — Company Profile

01 — The Company

A trading house that
became a mill.

Humayun Ibrahim Textile has been in the textile trade since 1996. ENZO is its manufacturing arm — launched in 2014 to weave, under our own name, the cloth we had spent nearly two decades buying and selling.

That sequence matters, because it decides what we can promise. A trader can offer you what the market happens to hold. A mill can commit to a construction and hold it across reorders. We do the second, and we do it on a deliberately narrow range — four qualities, improved rather than multiplied.

Headquarters and trade are in Lahore, at ENZO Tower on Main Westwood Road, Thokar Niaz Baig. Production is in Faisalabad, Pakistan's textile manufacturing centre. Both wholesale buyers and individual customers are served from the same production — the same cloth, by the roll or by the piece.

02 — The Range

Four qualities.
One standard.

We do not carry fifteen articles. We carry four, and the work goes into making them better rather than making the list longer.

Construction is given as reed × pick / warp × weft count — the numbers that actually describe a cloth, rather than a trade name that can mean anything. Every quality below is woven on our own looms.

03 — How We Trade

By the roll,
or by the piece.

One production line serves both sides of the business. A wholesale buyer and a walk-in customer are buying the same cloth.

Wholesale

Manufacturer-direct, quoted against your construction. Minimum order 10,000 metres per quality. Send reed, pick, width and yarn count and we will come back with a quotation.

Retail

Finished fabric sold by the piece at ENZO Tower, Lahore. Shades are confirmed against the physical swatch rather than a screen.
