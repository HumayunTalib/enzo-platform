Buying Woven Fabric from Pakistan: Information for International Buyers

ENZO Textiles supplies woven suiting fabric to B2B buyers in Pakistan and abroad. This page explains who ENZO works with, what to prepare for an enquiry and how an order is agreed.

Buyer markets

United Kingdom

Who this is for

Wholesalers, menswear and uniform garment makers, brands and sourcing agents buying woven suiting by the roll or lot.

What to prepare

Composition and width, GSM tolerance, your preferred test methods, destination port and Incoterm.

GCC: UAE and Saudi Arabia

Who this is for

Wholesalers, traders, uniform suppliers and garment makers buying PV and blended woven fabric.

What to prepare

Shade reference (physical swatch or lab dip), roll length and packing, destination port or consignee, payment instrument preferred.

United States

Who this is for

Brands, importers and garment manufacturers that buy woven fabric against written specifications.

What to prepare

Fibre-content and labelling needs for your market, inspection method (for example the four-point system), roll length, delivery window.

European Union

Who this is for

Importers, brands and garment manufacturers with documented supplier requirements.

What to prepare

Composition, applicable ISO test methods, your restricted-substances requirements and the documents you need with the shipment.

Pakistan

Who this is for

Wholesalers, garment manufacturers and traders in Lahore, Faisalabad, Karachi and Peshawar.

What to prepare

Construction and quantity, whether you want ready stock or custom weave, delivery location.

Bangladesh

Who this is for

Garment manufacturers and traders sourcing woven suiting fabric.

What to prepare

Composition, construction, width, shade, quantity and delivery terms.

China

Who this is for

Sourcing agents and trading companies buying woven fabric on behalf of overseas clients.

What to prepare

Full written specification, reference sample if available, destination and the end-client's inspection requirements.

Import duties, labelling rules and product-compliance requirements differ by destination. Confirm them with your customs broker; tell ENZO your destination so the quotation covers the right documents.

How an international order is agreed

  1. Send the RFQ

    Complete the RFQ form with specification, volume and destination.

  2. Specification review

    ENZO checks the construction and asks for what is missing.

  3. Quotation & terms

    Price, timeline, Incoterm, payment terms and documents [VERIFY SPEC].

  4. Production & inspection

    Weaving, dyeing if required, independent final inspection, dispatch.

What to include in your RFQ

  • Composition and, if known, yarn counts (Ne) and EPI × PPI
  • Weave, width and target GSM with tolerance
  • Greige or dyed, shade reference, finish
  • Quantity in metres and whether ready stock or custom weave
  • Packing needs, destination port and Incoterm
  • Inspection method and any test standards you require

Use the specification sheet template to collect these in one place.

Frequently asked

Does ENZO export fabric?

ENZO supplies B2B buyers outside Pakistan. Shipping terms, documentation and payment terms are agreed per order and set out in the quotation.

What is the minimum order for export?

Minimum order quantity is confirmed per construction and order type in the quotation.

Can I get a reference sample before ordering?

Sampling is arranged per order. Ask for sample terms in your RFQ.

How do I know the quality before shipment?

ENZO carries out an independent final quality inspection before dispatch. If you need your own inspector or an inspection method such as the four-point system, say so in the RFQ.

Tell ENZO where the fabric is going

Include destination and documentation needs in your RFQ so the quotation covers them.
