PV Suiting Buyer's Reference Guide

Terms, formulas and questions for buying polyester-viscose suiting.

Request a quote

What PV suiting is

PVA woven fabric made from polyester and viscose fibres, usually blended in the yarn, used widely for men's unstitched suiting and uniforms.

Polyester contributes strength and crease recovery; viscose contributes a softer hand and absorbency. The blend ratio sets the balance, so it belongs in the specification. ENZO weaves PV constructions including 36/1, 37/1 and 26/1 PV counts, and custom constructions to order.

Terms

TermMeaning
NeEnglish cotton count; higher is finer. Ne 36 = 16.4 tex.
EPI / PPIWarp ends and weft picks per inch
GSMGrams per square metre
36/1 vs 36/2One single yarn of Ne 36 vs two plied (resultant about Ne 18)
GreigeWoven but not yet dyed or finished
SelvedgeThe finished edge of the fabric

Formulas

GSM = 23.25 × [ EPI × (1 + crimp) ÷ Ne warp + PPI × (1 + crimp) ÷ Ne weft ]
tex = 590.541 ÷ Ne
Cover factor K = Kw + Kf − Kw·Kf ÷ 28, Kw = EPI ÷ √Ne

Calculators: GSM, yarn count, cover factor.

What to specify

  • Polyester/viscose ratio
  • Warp and weft count with system and ply
  • EPI × PPI and basis
  • Width, GSM with tolerance and basis
  • Finish, shade reference and approval method
  • Inspection method and acceptance limit

Questions to ask any supplier

  • Which steps are done in-house and which are outsourced?
  • Who inspects before dispatch, and are they independent of production?
  • What is the minimum order and what sets the timeline?
  • What documents and test reports can be provided, and which are current?
