HS Code for Polyester Viscose Fabric (5515.11): How Woven Blend Fabrics Are Classified

By ENZO Textiles · Published {{PUB_DATE_H}} · 4 min read

If you import woven fabric, the supplier’s invoice and your customs declaration need a Harmonized System (HS) code. For a blend of polyester and viscose staple fibres, the usual starting point is HS 5515.11. The details depend on the exact composition, how the fabric is made and your country’s tariff. This guide explains the logic so you can brief your supplier and your customs broker. It is general information: classification is decided by the customs authority of the importing country.

The core code: 5515.11

Heading 5515 covers other woven fabrics of synthetic staple fibres. The subheading 5515.11 is of polyester staple fibres mixed mainly or solely with viscose rayon staple fibres. In the EU Combined Nomenclature the explanatory note describes it as woven fabrics containing predominantly, but less than 85%, polyester staple fibres by weight, mixed principally or solely with viscose staple fibres (Wisepim, EU CN 2026). The United States tariff uses the same six-digit structure under 5515.11 (HTS Hub).

The first six digits are harmonised internationally. Digits beyond that are set by each country or trading bloc, and duty rates are national. Your broker will add the extra digits for your destination.

How to decide between neighbouring headings

If the fabric is…Look at heading
Polyester staple, mixed mainly with viscose, polyester below 85%5515.11
Viscose (artificial staple) is the main fibre, i.e. exceeds the polyester5516 (woven fabrics of artificial staple fibres)
85% or more synthetic staple fibres5512
Polyester staple mixed mainly with cotton (poly-cotton)5513 or 5514, split by weight
Made from polyester filament yarn, not staple5407 (woven fabrics of synthetic filament yarn)

Poly-rayon fabric is commonly misclassified under 5516, which is for fabrics where artificial staple fibres predominate; if the rayon content exceeds the polyester content by weight, 5516 is likely correct, and polyester filament fabric belongs elsewhere (HS Code Atlas). Heading 5515 acts as a residual category for synthetic-staple woven fabrics not covered by more specific headings (HS Code Atlas). For poly-cotton headings, check the weight threshold that separates 5513 and 5514 in the current tariff text.

The practical lesson: the blend ratio, the fibre type (staple or filament) and the fabric weight can all change the code. A PV fabric at 65/35 and one at 40/60 polyester/viscose may sit in different headings, because which fibre predominates changes.

Other factors that change the full code

  • Finishing state. Subheadings distinguish, for example, unbleached or bleached fabric from printed (Wisepim) and dyed or yarn-dyed fabric. Yarn-dyed polyester-viscose fabrics may be classified in a different subheading from piece-dyed (Goods Across Borders).
  • Weave and use. Some national tariffs go on to split by weave (poplin, twill and so on) or by weight.
  • Origin and trade agreements. Duty depends on both the code and the origin of the goods.

What to give your supplier and broker

  1. The exact composition, by weight, for example 65% polyester / 35% viscose, written as in this guide to abbreviations.
  2. Confirmation that yarns are staple (spun), not filament.
  3. Finish state (greige, dyed, yarn-dyed or printed) and weight (GSM).
  4. Your destination country, so the broker can apply the correct national digits.

Ask your supplier to state the HS code they use on the invoice, but treat it as a proposal; the importer’s broker confirms the classification for the destination.

Frequently asked questions

What is the HS code for polyester viscose fabric?

HS 5515.11 for woven fabrics of polyester staple fibres mixed mainly or solely with viscose rayon staple fibres, where polyester predominates but is below 85%.

What if viscose is the larger share?

Fabrics where artificial staple fibres such as viscose predominate are classified under heading 5516 instead.

Is the HS code the same in every country?

The first six digits are harmonised. Further digits and duty rates are set by each country.

What is the HS code for polyester cotton fabric?

Woven polyester staple fabrics mixed mainly with cotton fall under headings 5513 and 5514, depending on weight. Confirm the exact subheading in your tariff.

Who decides the final classification?

The customs authority of the importing country. A customs broker can advise, and a binding ruling can be requested where the system allows.

Conclusion

For a standard polyester-majority PV woven fabric, start at 5515.11, rule out 5516 and the poly-cotton headings by checking which fibre predominates, and let your broker add the national digits. Precise composition on the specification is what makes this straightforward.
