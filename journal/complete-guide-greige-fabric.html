Greige Fabric · PILLAR GUIDE

Complete Guide to Greige Fabric

Cluster: Greige Fabric · Primary keyword: greige fabric guide

This is the complete reference for greige fabric — the woven cloth stage before dyeing and finishing, and the starting point for nearly every textile product. It pulls together everything covered in our greige-fabric cluster into one guide.

What greige fabric is

Greige fabric (pronounced "grey") is woven cloth as it comes off the loom — construction fully set, but not yet scoured, dyed, printed, or finished. See our full explainer: what is greige fabric?

How it's manufactured

Yarn preparation, warping, sizing, weaving, then inspection and winding — see the complete sequence in how greige fabric is manufactured, step by step.

Greige vs finished fabric

Weight, width, and hand all shift during dyeing and finishing, which is why specifications must always state which stage they refer to — see greige vs finished fabric: what actually changes.

Evaluating greige fabric quality

Since colour and finish aren't yet relevant, evaluation focuses on construction (reed and pick — see reed and pick explained) and weaving defects — see our list of common greige fabric defects: missing ends, broken picks, slubs, reed marks, weft bars, and width variation.

Buying greige wholesale

Sourcing greige direct from a mill gives buyers cost and finishing control at the price of managing dyeing and finishing as a separate step — see wholesale greige fabric: sourcing direct from the mill for when this trade-off makes sense.

The single most important habit for any greige buyer: confirm and check the construction (reed × pick) directly with a pick glass, rather than relying on the fabric's trade name — the construction, not the name, is what a mill is actually able to reproduce consistently order after order.

Frequently asked questions

Is greige fabric lower quality than finished fabric?

No — it's an earlier stage, not a lower grade. Construction quality is fully determined at the greige stage.

Where is most Pakistani greige fabric produced?

Faisalabad — see our piece on Faisalabad's textile industry for why the region concentrated this capacity.
