Wholesale Fabric From Pakistan: MOQ, Lead Times and What to Expect

Cluster: Sourcing Pakistan · Primary keyword: wholesale fabric Pakistan

If you haven't sourced from Pakistan before, the hardest part isn't finding suppliers — it's knowing what a normal answer looks like, so you can tell whether a quoted lead time and minimum are competitive or a sign you're talking to a trader rather than a mill.

Who you can buy from

LayerTypical MOQBest for
Mill / manufacturerThousands to tens of thousands of metresRepeat orders at volume
Trader / stockistSometimes hundreds of metresSmall or varied lots
Export house / agentVariesOne contact across several mills

Manufacturing is concentrated around Faisalabad; commercial offices are frequently based in Lahore or Karachi. That split is normal, not evidence of an intermediary.

Why MOQ exists

Two fixed costs drive it: loom set-up (the same whether you run 2,000 metres or 20,000) and dye lot size. This is why MOQ is often quoted per construction and per shade, not as one total. ENZO's wholesale minimum is 10,000 metres.

The sourcing sequence

  1. Send a full construction — warp count, weft count, ends per inch, picks per inch, width, target GSM.
  2. Receive quotation — reputable suppliers respond in days, not weeks.
  3. Request swatches.
  4. Approve a pre-production sample from the actual run.
  5. Confirm commercial terms.
  6. Production — weaving, then dyeing and finishing.
  7. Pre-shipment inspection — see our inspection checklist.
  8. Shipment and documentation.

Realistic lead times

Treat a quoted date as components, not one figure: sampling and approval, yarn procurement, weaving, dyeing and finishing (often least predictable), inspection and packing, freight. Two questions cut through an optimistic quote: does that date include dyeing and finishing? and does it assume the yarn is already in stock?

What a complete quotation contains

  • Full construction specification
  • Finished width — total or usable
  • Target GSM and basis (greige or finished)
  • GSM and width tolerances
  • MOQ per construction and per shade
  • Lead time, with stages identified
  • Payment and delivery terms
  • Inspection standard applied

Frequently asked questions

What is a typical MOQ for woven fabric from Pakistan?

Mills commonly set minimums in the thousands to tens of thousands of metres per construction; traders can go lower. ENZO's is 10,000 m.

Where is woven fabric actually manufactured?

Faisalabad is the principal manufacturing centre; commercial offices are often in Lahore or Karachi.
