  1. Convert counts to a common basis with the yarn count converter.
  2. Calculate GSM and cover factor for your construction.
  3. Compare two candidate constructions side by side.
  4. Estimate yarn consumption and cost with your own prices.
  5. Send the figures to ENZO with your RFQ.

Frequently asked

Are the calculators free?

Yes. They run in your browser, need no sign-up and send nothing to ENZO unless you press a button.

How accurate are the results?

Results are theoretical values from standard textile formulas using the numbers you enter. Real fabric varies with crimp, sizing, finishing and moisture, so confirm against a weighed sample.

Can I send a result to ENZO?

Yes. Each tool has a button that carries your inputs into the RFQ form or into a WhatsApp message.
