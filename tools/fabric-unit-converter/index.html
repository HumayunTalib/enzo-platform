Definition: Unit conversionFabric specifications mix metric and imperial units: metres and yards for length, GSM and oz/yd² for weight, inches and centimetres for width. These converters keep them consistent.

Formulas and definitions

Unit definitions

1 yard = 0.9144 m exactly · yards = metres ÷ 0.9144
1 oz/yd² = 33.9057 g/m² · (28.3495 g ÷ 0.836127 m²)
kg per metre = width (m) × GSM ÷ 1000 · kg = metres × kg per metre · metres = kg ÷ kg per metre
1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly

Assumptions and limits

  • Metres to kilograms uses nominal GSM and nominal width; real rolls vary with moisture, width and weight tolerance.
  • Always state in an order whether quantity is in metres, yards or kilograms and the weight basis (greige or finished).

Frequently asked

How many yards are in a metre?

1.0936 yards (1 yd = 0.9144 m).

How do I convert fabric metres to kilograms?

Multiply metres × width (m) × GSM ÷ 1000. 1,000 m at 58 in and 120 GSM is about 176.8 kg.
