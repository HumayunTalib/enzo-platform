Definition: Fabric costingWoven fabric cost is built from yarn used per metre, sizing, weaving, dyeing and finishing, adjusted for wastage. The result depends entirely on the prices you enter.

Formula

Warp cost/m = warp g/m ÷ 1000 × warp price per kg · Weft cost/m likewise
Sizing/m = warp g/m ÷ 1000 × sizing cost per kg · Weaving/m = rate per metre, or picks per metre ÷ 1000 × rate per 1,000 picks
Cost before wastage = yarn + sizing + weaving + dyeing/finishing + other
Cost per metre = cost before wastage ÷ (1 − wastage %) · per yard = per metre × 0.9144

Where it comes from

Yarn quantities come from the warp and weft consumption formulas. Wastage is treated as a share of fabric lost, so the saleable metre carries that cost: dividing by (1 − wastage) recovers it.

Assumptions and limits

  • Currency is whatever you type in; the tool does not convert.
  • No prices, conversion rates or wastage percentages are assumed.
  • Dyeing/finishing is entered per metre of finished fabric.
  • This is an estimating worksheet, not a quotation. Taxes, financing and margin are not included.
  • ENZO quotes custom constructions after reviewing the full specification.

Frequently asked

How is woven fabric cost calculated?

Yarn used per metre × yarn price, plus sizing, weaving, dyeing and finishing, then adjusted for wastage.

Why divide by (1 − wastage)?

If 5% of fabric is lost, the good 95% must carry the whole cost.
