Definition: Specification comparisonComparing two constructions means putting their weight, density, balance and yarn fineness side by side so that the trade-off between them is visible.

Formula

GSM = 23.25 × [ EPI ÷ Ne(warp) + PPI ÷ Ne(weft) ] (crimp not included)
K = Kw + Kf − Kw·Kf ÷ 28, with Kw = EPI ÷ √Ne(warp), Kf = PPI ÷ √Ne(weft)
Yarn weight per metre = GSM × width (m)

Where it comes from

The tool applies the same GSM and cover factor formulas used in the individual calculators to both fabrics and reports the differences.

Assumptions and limits

  • Weight classes (very light < 100, light 100–130, medium 130–170, medium-heavy 170–220, heavy ≥ 220 GSM) are ENZO working bands for comparison only; they are not an industry standard.
  • Crimp is ignored so both fabrics are compared on the same basis.
  • Use a lab-measured GSM for decisions. This tool is for early-stage comparison of constructions.

Frequently asked

How do I compare two fabric constructions?

Compare GSM, cover factor, EPI:PPI balance and yarn tex. A heavier, more densely covered build will usually feel firmer and hang with more weight.

Is a higher cover factor better?

Not always. It signals a tighter build, which suits some uses and not others.
