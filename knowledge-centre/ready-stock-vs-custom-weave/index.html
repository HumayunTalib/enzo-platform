Ready Stock vs Custom Weave: Choosing the Right Order Type for Bulk Fabric

By ENZO Textiles · Published 19 November 2026 · 2 min read

In this article

  2. What sets the timeline for custom orders
  3. Greige or dyed

Bulk fabric can be bought two ways: from stock that already exists, or woven to your specification. The right choice depends on how much of the specification you can compromise and how much time you have.

DefinitionReady stock is finished fabric already held, in the construction and colours available. Custom weave is fabric made to a buyer's specification: yarn is procured, woven and, if required, dyed.

The two options compared

What sets the timeline for custom orders

For a custom order the timeline is the sum of its stages, and each can be the slowest. At ENZO those stages are yarn procurement, weaving, dyeing through external partners if the order is dyed, and an independent final inspection before dispatch. ENZO states the timeline in the quotation for your specification, not as a general figure.

Greige or dyed

Greige fabric is woven but not yet dyed or finished. A buyer with their own dyeing arrangement may want greige; a buyer who wants fabric ready to cut will want it dyed. If you order dyed fabric, state the shade reference and how it will be approved, because colour is the commonest source of rejection.

A simple way to choose

  • If the construction and colour you need already exist in stock and the quantity fits, ready stock is the shorter route.
  • If you need a specific construction, count, width or shade, or a larger quantity, specify a custom weave.
  • If you are not sure, send the specification anyway and ask which route fits.

See the current ready-stock lot on the Ready Stock page, or write the specification for a custom weave using the specification guide. The fabric cost estimator helps you compare routes with your own prices.

Frequently asked questions

Is ready stock cheaper than custom weave?

Not necessarily. Ready stock saves time and avoids a production run; price depends on the quality and quantity, so compare quotations.

Can I change the colour on a ready-stock lot?

No. Ready stock is fabric already finished in the colours held. A different colour needs a custom run.

What decides the timeline of a custom order?

Yarn availability, dyeing, the weaving schedule and the final inspection. ENZO states the timeline in the quotation.
