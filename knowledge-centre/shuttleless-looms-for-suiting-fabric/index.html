Shuttleless Looms for Suiting Fabric: Air-Jet, Rapier, Projectile and Water-Jet Compared

By ENZO Textiles · Published 19 November 2026 · 2 min read

In this article
  1. The four types
  2. What it means for a buyer
  3. ENZO's loom shed

"Shuttleless" describes how the weft yarn crosses the warp, not a single machine. The four main systems differ in how they carry the weft, what yarns they suit and where their limits lie. Knowing the difference helps you ask a mill the right question.

DefinitionA shuttleless loom inserts the weft pick across the warp shed by a carrier other than a conventional shuttle: a projectile, a rapier, a jet of air or a jet of water.

The four types

TypeHow the weft crossesNotes from the source
ProjectileA small gripper projectile carries the weft across the shedThe projectile is described as about 90 mm long and 40 g
RapierA rigid or flexible rapier carries the weft across the shedAmong the most commercially successful systems
Air-jetA jet of compressed air carries the weftAmong the most commercially successful systems
Water-jetA jet of water carries the weftSuited only to hydrophobic yarns; maximum fabric width about 230 cm in the source

The source names projectile, rapier and air-jet as the most commercially successful types.

What it means for a buyer

  • Fibre. A water-jet loom needs a hydrophobic weft, so it is not an option for cotton or viscose in the weft. The other types are not restricted in the same way.
  • Width. Loom type and reed width limit the widths that can be woven. If your spec needs an unusual width, say so early.
  • Not the whole story. Loom type is one input. Yarn quality, preparation, settings and inspection decide what arrives.

Frequently asked questions

What is a shuttleless loom?

A loom that inserts the weft without a conventional shuttle: projectile, rapier, air-jet and water-jet systems are the main types.

Which shuttleless loom is used for cotton?

Water-jet looms are limited to hydrophobic yarns, so they are not used for cotton or viscose weft. The other three types can handle a wider range.
